# MBA Surveys — Initial Concept

### Exploring a faster way for student researchers to reach relevant respondents.

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Concept_archive-64748B)

</div>

## Problem statement

Creating a survey is easy; finding enough people who match the research criteria is not. Student researchers often rely on personal networks and generic group chats, which can lead to slow collection and biased samples.

This repository captures the initial product direction for a marketplace connecting survey creators with willing, relevant respondents.

## Hypothesis

If creators can describe the audience they need and respondents can discover worthwhile surveys that match their profile, both sides can reduce the friction of research participation.

## Intended MVP

- Creator and respondent accounts
- Researcher-defined targeting criteria
- External survey-link publishing
- A respondent discovery feed
- Response targets and per-response incentives
- Basic creator progress tracking

## Status and evolution

**Concept archive — no application code is committed in this repository.**

The implemented prototype now lives in [mbasurveys_lovable](https://github.com/nikhilkoyyada7868/mbasurveys_lovable), including authentication, profiles, survey creation, targeting, discovery, dashboards, and a Supabase data model.

This repository remains useful as the origin of the product idea and a pointer to the active prototype.
